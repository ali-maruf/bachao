Questions

Good to know

What's in a Surprise Bag?

Whatever the shop didn't sell today: bread, mishti, cake, biryani, groceries. You'll see photos of typical contents, and it's always worth at least three times what you pay.

Is the food safe to eat?

Yes. It's the same food the shop sold all day. Every partner's trade licence is checked, and every bag shows a best-before time set by the shop.

What if I can't make the pickup time?

Cancel up to 2 hours before the window for a full refund. If you miss the pickup, the bag can't be refunded because the shop kept it for you.

How do I pay?

bKash, Nagad or card. For your very first order you can pay cash at pickup.

What if something is wrong with my bag?

Send us a photo within 2 hours of pickup. If the food is spoiled or the shop cancelled, you get your money back.
